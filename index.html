🧭 Quick Airport & GoaMiles Guide for Mom & Dad: Mopa (GOX) vs. Dabolim (GOI)

Manohar Intl Airport, Mopa (GOX) CLOSEST TO MAPUSA

Located in Pernem, North Goa — direct NH-66 highway drive to Mapusa & Divya Home (~25–30 mins). Air India AI 9841 departs Mumbai T2 at 16:05 and lands at GOX at 17:20 (1h 15m non-stop), plus daily IndiGo & Akasa Air flights. GoaMiles counter is right outside Arrivals.

Goa International Airport, Dabolim (GOI) SOUTH / CENTRAL GOA

Located near Vasco da Gama (~55–65 mins to Mapusa). Air India AI 603 (05:10 & 15:40) and AI 2841 (22:00) fly non-stop from Mumbai T2 in 1h 10m, with Economy fares starting from ₹3,486. GoaMiles booking booth available at Arrivals.
